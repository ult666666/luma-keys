# Illustrated Library · 2.13

The Library is arranged around returning to a piece: a large painted piano scene, the full title, the saved bar and a clear Continue action. The previous statistics grid is removed from this view. Saved passage chips still open the precise repeating range, paused.

## Collection

Three original, locally bundled illustrations give the collection distinct moods: storm coast, sunlit room and moonlit conservatory with a sleeping cat. Appassionata uses the storm scene; Sunny Day / 晴天 uses the sunny room. Other titles use a matching night/storm/sun cue or a stable selection. These are atmospheric covers, not official artwork or transcriptions.

Cards retain full titles, composer, bar count, key and any review-needed notice. Descriptions, format, tempo, note count and practice history live under Details. Exact same-title arrangements get stable Arrangement N of M labels. Covers and Open buttons load the same score; neither starts playback automatically. Search also includes descriptions that are collapsed visually. A search with no matches offers Clear search.

## Companion and motion

The Library companion looks toward the pointer and answers a click, touch, Enter or Space with a short greeting. Its name and speech-bubble button open the existing conversation without sending a message. Gestures stop after the greeting. Live reduced-motion preferences remove decorative motion and artwork zoom. Offscreen companions stop idle effects.

Artwork has fixed geometry, gentle bounded hover/focus zoom, and no inherited brightness flash or press translation. A missing illustration has a decorative piano fallback. Artwork is excluded from the accessibility tree; the image button itself names the piece/action. Keyboard focus is visible. Space on Details opens the disclosure without triggering playback.
