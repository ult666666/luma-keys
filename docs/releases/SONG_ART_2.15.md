The Library now gives each of its eight musical works a distinct illustration. Different editions of the same work share a cover while retaining their full arrangement titles and independent progress.

These are original artistic interpretations, not official album covers or claims about the composers' intended stories. Unknown imports keep a neutral library scene until a cover is available. Selection uses recognized work identities and bounded title/artist aliases rather than random assignment or broad mood words.

The paintings retain their own colors in every companion room. The surrounding frame follows the selected room. Cards display the full 3:2 illustration, while the Continue panel uses a subject-specific focal point. Titles and practice controls stay outside the art; keyboard access, subtle hover movement and reduced-motion behavior are retained.
