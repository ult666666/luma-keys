# Library flow 2.25

The October 2 Library update preserves the installed 2.24 artwork, companion, scores, expressive playback and conversation features.

- Compact header and Continue card put more of the collection within reach.
- Search clears with its X button or Escape. Chinese and full-width text are normalized for matching.
- All pieces, Practised and Unplayed filters combine with search. Sort by recency, title or composer.
- Resume bars, saved passages, recent pieces, review, removal and per-piece menus retain their actions. A recent piece that restarts now says “Play again”; a bookmark at the last bar is no longer presented as proof of completion.
- App navigation uses a steady bar, moving selection indicator and short directional transitions. Rapid changes commit once in order. Dialogs and chat have a single coordinated entrance.
- Reduced Motion and active playback bypass view transitions. No score, recording, chat history or practice setting is migrated by this release.

Verification and installation results are recorded in STATUS.md and evidence/library-flow-2.25-verification.json. General model intelligence, physical MIDI and sound quality are outside this interface-only pass.
