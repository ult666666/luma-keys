# Companion picker · 2.21

A smaller picker puts the six companions together beside a playful preview. The poster, slogans, repeated room descriptions and practice advice have been removed. Each bilingual card has its own colour accents, a preview check and a separate Current marker.

Click a card to preview its colours. Tap the larger portrait for a short greeting; it does not play sound or send a chat message. Choose applies the companion and colours. Match piano sound also applies its Dry, Room or Hall setting; turn it off to keep your current sound. Closing or pressing Escape leaves your selection unchanged.

The greeting respects reduced motion. Keyboard focus, accessible labels and the current marker remain available. In short windows the character area scrolls while sound and Choose remain reachable. Opening the picker dismisses a previous notification so it cannot cover those controls.

The picker follows saved settings if they finish loading after it opens. Previewing does not alter your piece, position, hands, speed or repeat range. The model, piano engine and score files are unchanged from 2.20.

Installed on Desktop. Verification:472 unit/API tests, six native interaction groups, six window sizes and an independent visual review. All108 latest personal files remain unchanged. Details: `evidence/companion-picker-2.21-verification.json`.
