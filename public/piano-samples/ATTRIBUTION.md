# Piano sample attribution

Salamander Grand Piano V3, recorded by **Alexander Holm** from a **Yamaha C5** grand piano. Original source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3 .

FLAC distribution and SFZ v2 remapping by **kinwie**, with optional retuning by **Markus Fiedler** and attack-offset metadata from **Jeff Learman**: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano . Pinned revision: `3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404`.

Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . The complete upstream licence is included in `LICENSE.txt`.

Luma Keys selects six of the sixteen recorded velocity layers (1,4,7,10,13,16), renames files by MIDI pitch, and adds a manifest. Audio bytes are unchanged; no trimming, resampling, gain normalization or lossy recompression was performed. Layer-selection, tuning, envelopes and effects are app playback decisions. This is a Yamaha C5 recording, not a Steinway recording.

Each of the180 FLAC files is checked against its upstream Git blob hash. `manifest.json` contains SHA-256 checksums, per-file source URLs and decoded-memory estimates.
